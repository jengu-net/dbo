This section comes first, and the rest of the guide is written as what it acts
upon. That is the opposite of how a store is usually explained, and deliberate:
the records, the tenants and the faces are all good answers on their own, but
what makes them worth the constraints they carry is that reaching them is an
act with a purpose attached.

## What this chapter will cover

- what a process and a step are, and why the step rather than the person is the
  thing access is granted to
- the run you already made: the writes in the quick start each produced one,
  and it can be read back — so work is not something you opt into later
- what a step declares: the data it may reach, the role that may perform it,
  and what it produces
- mandatory steps, and what an incident is
- why a job table with a worker loop is this same thing rebuilt without the
  proof
