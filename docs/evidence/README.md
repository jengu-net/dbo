# Evidence

Measurements a decision in this specification rests on.

A page here is neither a plan nor a promise. It records what was counted, when,
and against what, so that a decision citing it can be argued with on its
numbers rather than on its conclusion — and so that a reader who thinks the
conclusion is wrong can find out whether the measurement or the reasoning is
the part they disagree with.

- [**FHIR search usage inventory**](search-usage-inventory.md) — every FHIR
  search interaction a production healthcare platform actually issues against
  its server, across roughly 206 call sites, snapshotted 2026-08-14. It is what
  the search tiers are sized against.
