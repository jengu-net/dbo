The engine holds objects, identity, custody, declared handling, history,
tenancy and erasure. None of those is clinical. A **face** is what turns that
into a server somebody's existing client can talk to.

## What exists

**[The FHIR face](fhir.md)** — R4 and R5, running side by side over one engine,
different tenants on different versions. Ids, `_history`, `$expand`, the
searches and the operations a client expects.

That is the list. It is one item, and this page says so rather than implying a
set that is about to grow: two faces of a single tenant at once, and faces for
domains outside the FHIR family, are what the founding requirement asks for and
what the configuration model does not yet allow.

## Why the distinction is worth keeping

If FHIR were the model rather than a face, R4 and R5 would need two stores —
they interpret subscriptions differently, over the same outbox, today. And the
next domain would need a fork rather than a configuration.

The module layout is that claim made checkable: `dbo-core` has no FHIR in it,
and `dbo-fhir-common` is a module of its own precisely because the store is not
only for FHIR.
